# Skills Map: Dima's installed skills inside a board meeting

The board does not reinvent work that one of Dima's skills already does well. When a step below
applies and the skill is installed in this surface, **load it and follow its method**. If it isn't
installed, apply the summary given here directly and note that in the brief.

Skills that interview Dima (the contracts skill) or that spend credits (LeadMagic, SerpApi) are
never run silently. List them as a recommended next step, or ask first.

## Core: used in every meeting

| Skill | Where | How |
|---|---|---|
| **gotcha** | Step 6b, stress test of the draft resolution | Run gotcha's full method on the draft resolution: the ten attack passes (assumptions, math, sequencing, people, cash, legal, market, capacity, pre-mortem, loopholes), triage into Fatal / Serious / Nagging, then research every Fatal and Serious gap. Every Fatal gap must be fixed in the resolution, or carried into the vote as a named risk with a tripwire. The Independent Director owns this step. |
| **stop-slop** | Before delivery | Final pass on the report text and every asset. Removes AI writing patterns. Also enforces no em dashes and Dima's banned words. |

## Research and facts (Step 2 intake, and directors during rounds)

| Skill | Use when | Owner |

| **leadmagic: decision-makers, contact-lookup, validate-email** | Naming the actual buyers at target accounts for an outbound plan. | CRO |
| **leadmagic: market-search** | Sizing the target market (how many companies and people match the ideal customer), and building the list a campaign would work. | CMO / CRO |
| **leadmagic: hiring-intent** | Timing signals: target companies hiring for roles the offer replaces or supports. | CRO |
| **leadmagic: ads-research** | Competitor ad creative on Google, Meta and LinkedIn, to see their messaging angles before ours are written. | CMO / Customer Voice |
| **leadmagic: check-credits, credit-guard, bulk-enrichment** | Always check credits (free) and follow credit-guard: free checks first, composite calls over chains, no duplicate lookups. Get Dima's OK before any bulk enrichment. | Chair |
