# Tools Map

Tool names differ by surface; use whichever version of the connector is present. **R** = read
(Free). **W** = write (Approve, unless a standing approval covers it).

| System | Used by | Read | Write actions (gated) |
|---|---|---|---|
| QuickBooks | Finance Ops, Analyst | P&L, cash flow, balance sheet, sales by customer and product, A/R aging, estimates, invoices | Create or send estimates, invoices, payment links, recurring invoices |
| SalesHubHQ (GoHighLevel) | Marketing Ops, Sales Ops | Contacts, pipelines, campaign stats | Workflows on, contact imports, sends, stage moves |
| Windsor.ai | Marketing Ops, Analyst | Ads, GA4, Search Console, social, CRM data | Campaign, budget, bid and audience changes; Instagram and Google Business Profile posts |
| Supermetrics | Analyst | Marketing data across 170+ sources | Campaign management actions |
| LeadMagic | Sales Ops | Credit balance (free), company and people research | Bulk enrichment and any credit spend beyond a small sample |
| Microsoft 365 (Outlook, Teams, SharePoint) | Everyone | Mail, calendar, files, chats | Sending mail or Teams messages (drafts are Free), calendar invites, file changes outside the team folder |
| Google Drive | Everyone | Files | Creating or sharing files (creating in the team folder is Free; sharing is Approve) |
| Zapier | Automation Engineer | Available actions and connections | Running write actions, enabling zaps |
| Canva / Adobe Express | Design Producer | Designs, brand kits | Designs are Free; publishing or exporting to a public place is Approve |
| GitHub | Automation Engineer | Repos, issues, PRs | Issues and branches are Free in AlphaCIS repos; merging to main is Approve |
| Fathom / Granola / Wispr Flow | Content, Sales Ops, Analyst | Meeting notes and transcripts | None |
| Dripify (via Mark or Zapier) | Sales Ops | Campaign stats when exported | Launching or changing campaigns |
