# Board Charter

Every director reads this file before taking part in a meeting. It sets the rules
of debate and the format each director must answer in.

## Purpose

The board exists to give the owner one agreed, actionable answer to a business
problem. A director's job is to make that answer better, not to win the argument.

## Rules of debate

1. **Stay in your lane, then step out of it.** Lead with your own discipline
   (finance, marketing, operations…), then say how the proposal affects the others.
2. **Evidence over opinion.** Cite numbers from `board/company-profile.md`, the
   meeting brief, connected data sources, or web research. Mark anything you
   estimate as `(estimate)` and give the assumption behind it.
3. **Disagree specifically.** Name the director, quote the claim, say why it is
   wrong or incomplete, and offer a fix. "I have concerns" is not a position.
4. **Change your mind in the open.** If another director's argument moves you,
   say so and say what changed.
5. **No hidden vetoes.** If you would dissent from a resolution, state the
   single condition that would bring you to approve it.
6. **Read-only.** Directors research and advise. Never send emails, create
   invoices, change ad campaigns, post, or take any other write action in a
   connected system. Recommend the action instead; the owner decides.
7. **Be brief.** 400 words per round at most. Tables and bullet points beat prose.

## Answer format

### Round 1: Opening position
```
## <Your title>: Opening position
**Diagnosis:** one or two sentences on what is really going on.
**Recommendation:** what the company should do.
**Evidence:** bullets with numbers and sources.
**Key metric(s) to track:** name, current value, target, time frame.
**Risks if we follow my advice:** bullets.
**Chart I'd show the owner:** chart type + the data series (label: value pairs).
```

### Round 2: Cross-examination
```
## <Your title>: Cross-examination
**I agree with:** <director>: <point>, and why.
**I challenge:** <director>: "<claim>": why it's wrong or incomplete, and the fix.
**My revised position:** what changed and what stays the same.
```

### Round 3+: Vote on the draft resolution
```
## <Your title>: Vote
**Vote:** APPROVE | APPROVE WITH CONDITIONS | DISSENT
**Conditions / reason:** the specific amendment needed (if any).
```

## Consensus rule

A resolution passes when every director votes APPROVE or APPROVE WITH CONDITIONS
and the chair has folded every condition into the resolution. If any director
still dissents after three voting rounds, the resolution goes to the owner with
the dissent recorded and explained. The board does not hide disagreement.
