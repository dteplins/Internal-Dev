# Client engagements

The board can work on a client's business as well as AlphaCIS's. Each client gets its own folder:

```
board/clients/<client-slug>/
  profile.md          the client's facts (copy _template/profile.md)
  files/              anything the client gave you: exports, decks, brand guide, logo
  meetings/           that client's board meetings and reports
```

Start one by saying, for example:

```
/board-meeting for Master Window Systems: build a campaign to get 20 new dealer leads a month
```

If the folder doesn't exist yet, the Chair creates it from the template. It then fills in what
it can from:
- AlphaCIS's QuickBooks billing for that client
- the client's website and public sources
- LeadMagic company research, when connected
- meeting notes in Fathom or Granola

Then it asks you for the gaps that matter, in one batch.

## Rules for client work
- **Confidential by client.** A client's data stays in its folder. Directors never quote one
  client's figures in another client's meeting or in AlphaCIS's own meetings.
- **The client's goal comes first.** Recommend AlphaCIS services only where they are genuinely
  the best option, and label them "AlphaCIS can deliver this" so Dima can see the upsell
  separately from the advice.
- **The client's numbers, not AlphaCIS's.** AlphaCIS's QuickBooks only shows what AlphaCIS
  bills the client, not the client's own revenue or margins. Ask for those, or label estimates.
- **Client-facing reports** are written to the client ("you"), in their vocabulary. They carry
  the client's name and logo, with "Prepared by AlphaCIS" small in the footer.
