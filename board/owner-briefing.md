## Strategic direction: AI and workflow automation

Dima is productizing AI automation as an AlphaCIS service line, with manufacturing as the lead vertical and a free Architecture Session as the core conversion point.

- **Target industries:** manufacturing (priority), accounting and CPA firms, dealership and automotive, healthcare
- **Not targeting:** defense contractors (decided Sept 2026). Earlier defense and aerospace exploration surfaced real constraints: ITAR limits on sending export-controlled data to commercial APIs, and CMMC uncertainty after DoD suspended Phase 2
- **Website:** homepage being repositioned to lead with AI and workflow automation, plus a dedicated AI workflows page; Google Business Profile still leads with managed IT
- **Free offer:** a self-serve "Sorter" tool where a prospect describes a process and gets a verdict, the build shape and what it takes; email-gated through SalesHubHQ
- **Conversion goal:** a booked Architecture Session. The prospect walks through their current process, AlphaCIS architects the full automation for free, and paid implementation and support follow
- **Webinars:** a temporary tactic, now secondary to the Sorter; content must not be tied to them in a way that dates it
- **Video:** educational long-form plus short CTA clips across YouTube, LinkedIn, Reels, website and email; Dima on camera with screen recordings of real automations; first batch is manufacturing only. Goal is industry use cases feeding the funnel, not becoming a YouTuber
- **Capacity:** 3 to 5 Architecture Sessions per week; 2 to 4 video pieces per month, cut into more
- **Internal tooling:** a planned multi-agent campaign builder (orchestrator plus specialist agents for brochure, landing page, email sequence, phone script and strategy), built with Claude Code subagents, AlphaCIS campaigns only

## Go-to-market and outbound sales

The AlphaCIS Outbound Playbook is built; as of Sept 2026 Mark's outreach for the automation offer had not yet started.

How a lead moves through the system:

1. **Find:** LinkedIn automation (Dripify), contact enrichment (LeadMagic), email campaigns, and a Reddit lead-monitoring channel (RSS feeds into a Google Sheet, Claude drafts replies, the appointment setter posts them)
2. **Capture:** the Sorter tool and other lead magnets, email-gated in SalesHubHQ
3. **Call:** Mark, the full-time appointment setter, runs cold and warm call scripts
4. **Book:** the ask is always the free Architecture Session with Dima, no lighter first step and no downsell
5. **Close:** paid implementation, support, and managed IT

Decisions already locked:

- LinkedIn invites use short, punchy Atlanta-network copy
- Automated post-likes removed
- Re-engagement campaigns run from Dima's side, not Mark's
- Reddit: owners of 5 to 50 employee businesses in vertical niches; no posting in r/msp or competitor subs; no spammy behavior
