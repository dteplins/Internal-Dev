# Playbooks

A playbook tells the board how to work a recurring type of problem: the steps, who to seat,
which skills and data to use, and what extra charts and deliverables the report needs.
The Chair picks a playbook at intake when one fits; otherwise the standard meeting runs.

| Playbook | Use for |
|---|---|
| `marketing-campaign.md` | Campaigns, lead generation, launches, outbound pushes, for AlphaCIS or a client |

To add one, copy the structure of `marketing-campaign.md`. Give it:
- who to seat
- the steps with an owner each
- the tools and skills to use
- the execution package to build after the vote
- the report additions
