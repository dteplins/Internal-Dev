How a meeting runs

From your question to a report you can act on

Directors answer alone first so the debate starts from independent views. Nothing is sent, posted or changed in your systems; everything the board produces is a draft for you.

  1. Intake

    The Chair pins a measurable goal, pulls QuickBooks and other connected data, and writes the brief. At most three questions for you.

  2. Seat the board

    CFO and Independent always sit. The problem type adds 3 to 5 more.

  3. In parallel

    Opening positions

    Each director answers alone with a diagnosis, recommendation, evidence and a chart.

  4. In parallel

    Cross-examination

    Directors challenge each other by name. The Chair maps agreements, conflicts and open questions.

  5. Draft resolution

    Decision, plan with owners and dates, financials, KPIs, kill-switch, confidence.

  6. gotcha

    Stress test

    The Independent Director runs gotcha: ten attack passes, research on every serious gap. Fatal gaps get fixed before the vote.

  7. Up to 3 rounds

    Vote

    Approve, approve with conditions, or dissent. Dissent that survives three rounds is shown to you, never hidden.

  8. Build asks

    Execution package

    Campaign drafts from your marketing skills, reviewed by CMO, Customer Voice and Independent, then cleaned by stop-slop.

  9. Report

    Verdict first, at least three charts, a diagram, the vote, risks, stress test and next steps. Five lines in chat.

Who sits when
